## Top 5 Premium Mech-Keyboards for Gamers - Expert Picks

Gaming mechanical keyboards are known for being more precise, long-wearing, and may be more comfortable to use over long periods as they require less pressure from your fingers to spring. When it comes to gaming, precision and speed are everything -- so it's no wonder many gamers opt to go mechanical if they can.

## Is a gaming mechanical keyboard really worth it?

Using a keyboard is, of course, down to subjective experience and personal preference. However, if you like a kit with a more stable, quality feel, it might be worth the investment. 

It should also be noted that properly-cared mechanical keyboards can last far longer than their membrane counterparts. 

## Are gaming mechanical keyboards really better for gaming?

If you are an enthusiastic gamer, the consensus is that gaming mechanical keyboards can allow you to respond faster and more accurately -- which can make or break a speed run or tournament. When you need to use multiple keys, and quickly, rubber-based boards may be inaccurate or sluggish in comparison. 

Moreover, with proper care, they'll last longer. Therefore, you'll receive enhanced game play and better durability than traditional keyboards.

## Is it harder to type on a gaming mechanical keyboard?

Whether you're trying out a different keyboard format or are learning to touch type, using a gaming mechanical keyboard can be a learning curve. In itself, your accuracy and speed are based on your typing skills rather than the kit you use, and so -- at least, at the beginning -- it might be harder to type in the beginning. 

However, if you enjoy the audible "click" of most mechanical keyboards and the feel of the individual springs, it might, at the least, be more enjoyable.
