 First, swipe down from the top of the screen twice to expand the Quick Settings, then tap the gear icon to open the Settings.

## How to Disable Haptic Feedback on a Samsung Galaxy

 First, swipe down once from the top of the screen and tap the gear icon to open the Settings.

![Tap the gear icon.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/2021-12-07_16-38-15.png) 

 Now go to the "Sounds and Vibration" section.

![Select &quot;Sounds and Vibration.&quot;](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/2021-12-07_16-38-41.png)

 Scroll down to "System Sound/Vibration Control."

![Tap &quot;System Sound/Vibration Control.&quot;](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/2021-12-07_16-39-31.png) 

 The bottom section of toggles is for vibration. Toggle on or off any of the things you'd like to change.

![Toggle off the switches.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/2021-12-07_16-40-34.png) 

 Note that you can disable vibration for the Samsung Keyboard here, but if you use another keyboard, you'll have to do it from that app's settings.

 Your phone will now be vibration and haptic feedback-free. Haptic feedback is nice if your phone has a good vibration motor, [but it can be annoying](https://vp-tips.techidaily.com/updated-2024-approved-editscreen-pro-windows-8/) if it doesn't feel good.
