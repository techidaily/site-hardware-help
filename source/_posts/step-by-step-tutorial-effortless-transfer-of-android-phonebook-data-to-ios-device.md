 To transfer contacts from Android to iPhone, you can install Apple's official Move to iOS app on your Android phone. You can also export your Android contacts to a VCF file and then import them in the iPhone's Contacts app. With some mobile providers, you can transfer contacts via the SIM card.

##  Transfer Contacts From Android to iPhone With "Move to iOS"

 Apple has an official "[Move to iOS](https://www.anrdoezrs.net/links/3607085/type/dlg/sid/UUhtgUeUpU209215/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.apple.movetoios&hl=en)" app that's available in the Google Play Store for Android devices. This is a great tool, but there's a catch---it only works while setting up a new iPhone. You can't use this if you already went through the initial iPhone setup process.

 First, [download the Move to iOS app](https://www.anrdoezrs.net/links/3607085/type/dlg/sid/UUhtgUeUpU209215/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.apple.movetoios&hl=en) on your Android device. You'll be asked to grant the app permission to access your location and send notifications in order to function properly.

![Accept the permission requests.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/2023-02-14_10-08-16.png) 

 Next, on your iPhone, go through the setup and select "Move Data from Android" when the option appears. You'll be given a six-digit code to enter in the "Move to iOS" app on your Android device.

![Tap &quot;Continue&quot; to find your code.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/2023-02-14_10-08-29.png) 

 After entering the six-digit code, you'll see a number of things you can transfer to your iPhone. Make sure to select "Contacts" and tap "Continue."

![Select &quot;Contacts&quot; and &quot;Continue.&quot;](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/2023-02-14_10-18-10.png)

##  Sync Contacts From Android to iPhone With Google

 If the contacts on your Android phone are backed up to your Google account, you don't have to "transfer" them to your iPhone. We can simply add your Google account to the iPhone and sync them over.

 First, open the "Settings" app on your iPhone.

![Launch the Settings app.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/2021-07-30_11-57-53.png)

##  Move Contacts From Android to iPhone With a File

 One overlooked method for transferring contacts is exporting them as a file. You can create a VCF file that contains all of your contacts' information from your Android device and import it into the iPhone's Contacts app.

 If you don't already have it, install [Google's "Contacts" app](https://www.anrdoezrs.net/links/3607085/type/dlg/sid/UUhtgUeUpU209215/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.contacts) on your Android phone. In the app, go to the "Fix & Manage" tab and select "Export to File."

![Select &quot;Export to File.&quot;](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/2023-02-14_11-02-42.png) 

 You'll be brought to a file manager, and you can select where you want to save the .vcf file. Tap "Save" when you're done.

![Save the file when you choose a location.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/2021-07-30_12-51-24.png) 

 Now, you need to get that .vcf file onto your iPhone somehow. You can email it to yourself, upload it to a cloud storage service such as Google Drive, or send it over Bluetooth. Do whatever you're comfortable with.

 After you get it onto your iPhone, open the .vcf file once it's there.

![Open the file on your iPhone.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/2021-07-30_12-38-11.png)

##  Transferring Contacts From Android to iPhone via SIM and eSIM

 Contacts can be saved to your SIM---both physical SIM cards and [eSIM](https://facebook-video-footage.techidaily.com/unveiling-the-hidden-pathways-to-earning-on-youtube-for-2024/)\---if your mobile provider supports it. This way, your contacts are always attached to your phone number, and you don't need to sync them to a cloud service.

Related: [What Is an eSIM, and How Is It Different From a SIM Card?](https://facebook-video-footage.techidaily.com/unveiling-the-hidden-pathways-to-earning-on-youtube-for-2024/) 

 Saving contacts to a SIM is not possible with every app. For example, Samsung's Contacts app supports the feature, while Google's does not. We'll be using the Samsung Contacts app---if your contacts app supports the feature, look for similar options.

 With your SIM card or active eSIM in your device, open the app and tap the [hamburger menu](https://review-topics.techidaily.com/recover-your-contacts-after-oppo-reno-8t-has-been-deleted-by-fonelab-android-recover-contacts/) icon on the left side, then select "Manage Contacts" from the menu.

![Select the menu icon and tap &quot;Manage Contacts.&quot;](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Screenshot_20230214_121244_Contacts.png) 

 Next, select "Export Contacts."

![Select &quot;Export Contacts.&quot;](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Screenshot_20230214_121140_Contacts.png) 

 Select your SIM from the "To" section and tap "Export."

![Tap &quot;Export.&quot;](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Screenshot_20230214_121225_Contacts.png) 

 Now move your SIM card or eSIM to your iPhone and open the "Settings" app.

![Launch the Settings app.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/2021-07-30_11-57-53.png)
