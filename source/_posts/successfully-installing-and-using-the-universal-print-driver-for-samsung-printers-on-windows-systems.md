1. Go to **[Samsung Support center](https://shop-links.co/link/?exclusive=1&publisher_slug=itechdaily19598&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.samsung.com%2Fsemiconductor%2Fminisite%2Fssd%2Fproduct%2Fconsumer%2F960evo%2F)**  .
